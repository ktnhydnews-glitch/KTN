The Purpose

A network built to keep the memory of Karbala alive.

01

Every Majlis, Witnessed

From the Ashoor Khana of Golconda to the galliyan of Yakutpura, we carry the azadari of Hyderabad to every screen — uninterrupted, reverent, and live.

02

For the Distant Momineen

For those who cannot be present, distance should never mean absence. Our broadcasts let Shia viewers worldwide grieve, remember, and participate as one.

03

Preserving the Voice of Aza

Noha, soz, and marsiya — the sound of Karbala remembered. We archive and stream these traditions so they endure for the generations to come.
